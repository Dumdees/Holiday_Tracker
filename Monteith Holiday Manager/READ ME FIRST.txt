MONTEITH PERSONAL CARE - HOLIDAY MANAGER
========================================

HOW TO OPEN IT
--------------
If you used the installer: double-click the "Monteith Holiday Manager"
icon on your Desktop or in the Start menu. That is all you need to do.

If you have the folder instead:
1. Double-click the file called "Monteith Holiday Manager".
   It opens in your web browser (Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome).
   That is all - there is nothing to install.

2. Prefer a tidy window without the browser bar at the top?
   Double-click "Open Monteith Holiday Manager" instead.
   If Windows asks "Are you sure you want to open this file?", click Run.

TIP: To keep it handy, right-click the file, choose "Send to", then
"Desktop (create shortcut)". You can then open it from your desktop.

THE FIRST TIME
--------------
A short welcome screen asks for your holiday year and your teams.
You can choose "Explore with sample data" to have a look around first,
then clear it from Settings > Advanced when you are ready to add real carers.

WHERE IS MY INFORMATION KEPT?
-----------------------------
Everything you enter is saved automatically on this computer, inside the
web browser you use to open the app. Nothing is sent over the internet.

Because of that:
 - Always open the app in the SAME browser (for example, always Edge).
   Each browser keeps its own separate copy.
 - Do not "clear browsing data" in that browser without saving a backup
   first, or you could lose your holiday records.
 - To move to a new computer: Settings > Backup > "Save a backup file",
   copy that file across, then use "Restore from a backup file".

BACKUPS
-------
Go to Settings > Backup and click "Save a backup file" about once a week
(the app reminds you on the Home screen). Keep the file somewhere safe,
such as OneDrive or a memory stick.

NEED TO START AGAIN?
--------------------
Settings > Advanced > "Clear all data".
