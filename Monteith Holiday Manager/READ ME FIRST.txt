MONTEITH PERSONAL CARE - HOLIDAY MANAGER
========================================

HOW TO OPEN IT
--------------
Double-click the "Monteith Holiday Manager" icon on your Desktop or in the
Start menu (if you used the installer), or the "Monteith Holiday Manager"
program in this folder. It opens in its own window. That is all.

If Windows says "Windows protected your PC" the first time, click
"More info" and then "Run anyway".

TIP: To keep it handy, right-click the file, choose "Send to", then
"Desktop (create shortcut)". You can then open it from your desktop.

THE FIRST TIME
--------------
A short welcome screen asks for your holiday year and your teams.
You can choose "Explore with sample data" to have a look around first,
then clear it from Settings > Advanced when you are ready to add real carers.

WHERE IS MY INFORMATION KEPT?
-----------------------------
Everything you enter is saved automatically on this computer, in your own
Windows account. Nothing is sent over the internet. It stays put when the
program is updated.

To move to a new computer: Settings > Backup > "Save a backup file", copy
that file across, then use "Restore from a backup file" on the new one.

BACKUPS
-------
Go to Settings > Backup and click "Save a backup file" about once a week
(the app reminds you on the Home screen). Keep the file somewhere safe,
such as OneDrive or a memory stick.

NEED TO START AGAIN?
--------------------
Settings > Advanced > "Clear all data".

This app was made for Monteith Personal Care.
