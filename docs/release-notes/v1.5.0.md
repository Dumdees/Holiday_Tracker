## Monteith Holiday Manager 1.5.0

**Care Empire has been rebuilt.** The tea-break game is now a proper little strategy game about running a home-care service, and it looks the part.

- **Two sides to balance.** The work you have taken on and the team who deliver it both have to grow. Whichever is behind is worth more for your money, and a strip under the street tells you which one, in a sentence. Nothing in the game can ever cost you income: every purchase is worth having, and the shop says exactly how much.
- **Upgrades worth thinking about.** They do different jobs: key safes that make every carer quicker, a rota app in everyone's hand, bonuses that grow the more of the work your team can cover, cheaper prices, automation, and boosters that make every tenth purchase worth even more. Each tile carries one figure on its face – "+40%", "−20% to buy", "×3 a tap" – so two upgrades can be compared at a glance, and hovering says what it does, when it pays for itself and what you will see change on the street.
- **Three big decisions each run.** Who you work for, how you grow, and what you are known for. Each choice is a different shape rather than a different number – a bonus, a discount, or a flat rate that also rewards doing the visits yourself – so there is no right answer: it depends on how you have played.
- **It never runs out.** Every stage past Mars brings new rungs to climb, new kit, and a Legacy perk that costs twice the last one. The stages get a little longer each time instead of stopping dead.
- **Your own visits always count.** Tapping the street finds the nearest door that is ready, and the late upgrades make one visit of yours worth a real share of the whole business.
- **Everything you buy shows up.** Key safes on doors, care folders on doorsteps, sensor lights, sat navs and liveried cars and an electric fleet, coordinators and supervisors and nurses walking the patch, every branch and academy on the skyline, and an office that wears its sign, its rating sticker, its certificate, its noticeboard and its bunting.
- **Real home care.** Care packages, direct payments, council contracts, hospital discharge, framework places, NHS-funded care, nurse-led teams, the Care Certificate, guaranteed hours, and turning down calls too short to do properly. Your rating goes up when you invest in the things a good service invests in.
- **Nobody loses progress.** Older saved games are brought across onto the new street.

**To install or update:** download the file ending in **Setup.exe** below and double-click it. It updates an existing copy in place.
