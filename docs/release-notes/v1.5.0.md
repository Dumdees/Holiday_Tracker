## Monteith Holiday Manager 1.5.0

**Care Empire has been rebuilt.** The tea-break game is now a proper little strategy game about running a home-care service, and it looks the part.

- **Two sides to balance.** The work you have taken on and the team who deliver it both have to grow. Whichever is behind is worth more for your money, and a strip under the street tells you which one, in a sentence.
- **Upgrades worth thinking about.** Ninety-nine of them, and they do different jobs: key safes that make every carer quicker, a rota app in everyone's hand, bonuses that only apply while your team can cover the work, cheaper prices, automation, and boosters that make every tenth purchase worth even more. Each one says what it does, what it costs, when it pays for itself, and what you will see change on the street.
- **Three big decisions each run.** Who you work for, how you grow, and what you are known for. There is no right answer: it depends on how you have played.
- **Everything you buy shows up.** Key safes on doors, care folders on doorsteps, sensor lights, liveried cars, coordinators and supervisors and nurses walking the patch, branch offices and academies on the skyline, and an office that wears its sign, its rating sticker, its certificate, its noticeboard and its bunting.
- **Real home care.** Care packages, direct payments, council contracts, hospital discharge, framework places, NHS-funded care, nurse-led teams, the Care Certificate, guaranteed hours, and turning down calls too short to do properly. Your rating goes up when you invest in the things a good service invests in.
- **Nobody loses progress.** Older saved games are brought across onto the new street.

**To install or update:** download the file ending in **Setup.exe** below and double-click it. It updates an existing copy in place.
