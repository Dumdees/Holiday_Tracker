## Monteith Holiday Manager 1.2.0

**New: Care Empire**, a light-hearted tea-break game in the menu. Your street comes to life: carers walk from the Monteith office to the houses, residents wave at the door, care cars drive by, lamps come on at night and the odd shower passes over. Tap a house to send your star carer dashing over. Collect the payments, then hire your real team, buy care cars, rota apps, offices and hubs, and grow from one street to the whole world, then orbit, the Moon and Mars, with the scenery changing as you go. Expanding to the next stage starts the run again but earns Legacy Stars that make everything faster, forever. Catch the prismatic carers walking down the street and the thank-you cards floating down, collect achievements (with fireworks), and your team keeps working while the app is closed.

The game is completely separate from your real holiday records. Hide it any time under *Settings → General*.

**To install or update:** download the file ending in **Setup.exe** below and double-click it. It updates an existing copy in place.
