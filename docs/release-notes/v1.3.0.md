## Monteith Holiday Manager 1.3.0

**Care Empire: homes matter now.** You start with a single client home and buy more from the shop. Every carer needs a home to visit and a home takes one carer at a time, so a team without enough homes waits at the office (the street shows who is waiting, and the shop says how many homes you are short). A home you have just visited yourself needs a moment before the next knock, with a little timer over the door, so growing the street is the way to earn faster. New badges for caring for 10 and 100 homes.

**To install or update:** download the file ending in **Setup.exe** below and double-click it. It updates an existing copy in place.
