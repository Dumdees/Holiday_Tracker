## Monteith Holiday Manager 1.0.0

The first release of the holiday manager for carers at Monteith Personal Care.

**To install on a Windows computer:** download the file ending in **Setup.exe** below, double-click it and click Next until it finishes. A *Monteith Holiday Manager* icon appears on your Desktop and in the Start menu. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click *More info* then *Run anyway*.

Prefer not to install anything? `Monteith-Holiday-Manager.zip` has the same app as a folder: open it, drag the folder to your Desktop and double-click `Monteith Holiday Manager` inside it. Everything stays on your computer either way; nothing is sent over the internet.

**What's in it**

- Home screen showing who's off today and this week, what's coming up, and anything that needs your attention.
- Calendar with a colour-coded chip for each carer, a year overview and a week list.
- A searchable list of carers with their holiday entitlement, days used, days left, and a profile for each person.
- Add holidays for one carer or many at once, with clashes explained in plain English before you confirm.
- Remove holidays in bulk, approve or decline requests, and undo any change.
- Reports and charts: usage per carer, leave by month and type, team cover heat map, sickness, and printable summaries.
- UK bank holidays for Scotland, England & Wales or Northern Ireland, plus your own closure days.
- Backup and restore to a single file, with a gentle weekly reminder.
